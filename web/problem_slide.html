Problem Context // Market Analysis

The Battle is Asymmetric.

E-commerce platforms use dynamic pricing engines and AI to maximize margins. Malaysian shoppers are fighting back with static, passive wishlists.

Threat #1

Unpredictable Price Fluctuations

Sellers on platforms like Shopee and Lazada often raise prices 20–30% exactly 14 days before mega-sales, making it hard for users to know the best time to buy.

User Impact Fake Discounts
Threat #2

Delayed Currency Adjustments

Imported product prices fluctuate with USD changes, but local prices reflect these changes only after 48 hours, causing uncertainty in purchasing decisions.

User Impact Missed Buy Window
Root Cause

Passive Wishlist Limitations

Current wishlists act as “dumb containers” that do not track price history, analyze trends, or predict future price changes, leaving users without insights.

Seller Tool AI Pricing Engine
Buyer Tool Passive Wishlist
